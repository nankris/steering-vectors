## Issue and evidence
**DISPROVEN was structurally near-guaranteed by the ocean Y = 0 pp ceiling.** Any confirmatory loss with bootstrap LB > 0 (i.e. ~2+ net extra failures out of 200) fails H1; with baseline useful 95.5%/90.5%, a zero-loss ceiling is unachievable for any real intervention. Both ocean FAILs are exactly this (4B loss 8.0 [3.5,12.5] vs Y=0). The authors registered it knowingly ("we retain it rather than repair the hypothesis") — honest, but the headline verdict is mostly an artifact of a degenerate 16-item pilot.
